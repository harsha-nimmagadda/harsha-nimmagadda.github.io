The 3 Golden Rules for Supporting Competitive Exam Students

1. Avoid Peer Comparisons

Never ask "How much did Sharma-ji's son get?". In competitive exams, comparing against classmates breeds defensive silence. Compare your child only against their own previous mock score.

2. Protect 7 Hours of Sleep

Calculation slips (-12 marks in WTM-21) are 3x more likely when students sleep under 6 hours before a 3-hour Sunday morning exam. Sleep is a performance multiplier.

3. Focus on Process, Not AIR

Praise her for sitting through 18 of 20 tests and completing homework. Ranks fluctuate with paper difficulty, but test discipline guarantees a top college seat.
